This wiki is edited through git PRs, not a click-to-edit box - see [Goodbye, MediaWiki](/workshop/goodbye-mediawiki/) for why.

## Articles

Generative AI allowed however only as a tool. No single-shot generation. A page should be yours, roughly 80/20 in favor of your own writing. This wiki is about you and not a LLM retelling the story. For example AI touching it up rather than writing it. Such as fixing grammar, spelling, or formatting.

If you used AI on a page, add `ai: true` to its tags. The page will then show a short note saying AI helped write it.

## Code

More freedom here. Use AI to write, refactor, or debug. The bar isn't lower, it's just different: you need to understand what your PR does and be able to stand behind every part of it. If you can't explain a change or defend a decision it makes, it isn't ready to submit, regardless of who typed it. See [`CLAUDE.md`](https://github.com/CodingTwist/slabwiki/blob/main/CLAUDE.md) in the repo for the conventions any code, AI-assisted or not, should follow.
