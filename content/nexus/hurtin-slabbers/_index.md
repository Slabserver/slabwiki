**Hurtin' Slabbers** is a parkour platformer made by maketues. It started life as a survival build on Slab. The Nexus version keeps the game exactly as it was, but adds instancing, protected blocks and a proper save system, so your progress carries over between sessions.

## The Game

The game is linear: each level is a run of obstacles that tests your Minecraft movement. Most levels have more than one route through them, and a leaderboard tracks your time against other players.

There are 8 levels, ending in a boss fight. A full run takes around 90 minutes depending on skill, but you don't need to finish it in one go.

## Checkpoints

Checkpoints sit between levels. To save one, click the sign **first**, then stand on the campfire until you die. A chat message confirms it worked.

Whenever you die, you respawn at your last checkpoint.

## Saving and Quitting

You can leave at any time. The server remembers which level you were on and your time so far. When you come back, it puts you at your last checkpoint, just as if you had died.
