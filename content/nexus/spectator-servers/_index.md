The **Spectator Servers** are a read-only archive of Slab's past worlds. Join, fly around and explore the server's history at your own pace.

## Worlds

These worlds are currently in the archive:

* Season 1
* Season 2
* Season 3
* 2026 April Fools

## How It Works

When you join, you get a menu item in your inventory. Use it to pick a world and teleport there, then explore however you like.

- You can toggle flight on and off.
- No-clip lets you pass through blocks to get inside builds.
- You can't place or break blocks. Each world is frozen exactly as it was left.
- Mobs are frozen in place too.
- You can read any lectern books left in the world.

## Getting Around

Use the menu item to switch dimensions or jump to preset locations. Otherwise, fly anywhere you like within a world.

See [Technical details](technical-details/) for the plugin list and implementation notes.
